# Sales Data Visualization and Storytelling using Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing sales data and creating interactive visualizations using Power BI. The dashboard presents key business insights through various charts and a summary storyboard to support data-driven decision-making.

## Tool Used
- Power BI

## Dashboard Contents
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Sales by Segment
- Regional Sales Analysis
- Top 20 Products by Sales
- Total Profit Overview
- Storyboard with Business Recommendations

## Key Insights
- West region generated the highest sales.
- East region was the second-highest performing region.
- South region recorded the lowest sales.
- Technology was the highest-selling product category.
- Consumer segment contributed the largest share of total sales.
- A small number of products generated a significant portion of overall revenue.

## Business Recommendations
- Increase marketing efforts in low-performing regions.
- Focus on expanding sales in the Home Office segment.
- Promote high-performing Technology products.
- Monitor product performance regularly to optimize inventory and sales strategies.

## Outcome
This Power BI dashboard provides a clear and interactive view of sales performance, helping identify trends, compare business segments, and support informed business decisions.
